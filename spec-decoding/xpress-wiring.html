How the XPress drafter is wired into spyre-inference

XPress speculative decoding on Spyre: the five-stage stack starting at #1394 (merged), stages 2–5 drawn from ani300/spyre-inference#4 (5234eea) on vLLM 0.32.0.

Stage by stage

  • 1, #1394 (merged): XPressRefinerHead, checkpoint.py, the vendored-code canary.
  • 2, verification: host rejection sampler kernels, non-causal attention recording, widened logits row buckets, host draft-id copy.
  • 3, Qwen3 through DFlash: models/qwen3_dflash.py, SpyreDFlashProposer, the config bridge, registry entries, the runner hooks, single-request serving.
  • 4, batching: up to four concurrent requests, draft batches padded to 1, 2 or 4.
  • 5, resident selection: opt-in device feedback and device shortlist selection (FP16VocabularySelector), behind SPYRE_XPRESS_DEVICE_*.
